    Microsoft apps to restrict

    Windows Explorer, Windows Security, Task Manager, shutdown/restart and essential Windows processes are never touched.

    Ending a prayer session early

    To end a real prayer session early, you scan a QR code with your phone and photograph your prayer mat (your phone and PC must be on the same Wi-Fi). With an Anthropic API key saved, the photo is checked by AI to confirm it shows a prayer mat. Without a key, any photo is accepted.

    Notifications, sound & startup

    About

    Prayer Pause calculates your prayer times, then pauses selected apps for a short prayer session at each one.

    It works by asking the chosen apps to close while a session runs. It changes no Windows settings or services, so quitting the app or finishing the session always restores normal access. It cannot guarantee every program is blocked.

    Prayer times by the open-source Adhan library. Version 0.4.0.
